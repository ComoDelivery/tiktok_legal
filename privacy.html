AutoVid – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

1. Overview

AutoVid ("we", "our", "the Service") is a video scheduling and publishing platform for content creators. This Privacy Policy explains how AutoVid collects, uses, and protects data when you use our Service in connection with TikTok's API.

2. Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to AutoVid, we access the following data through TikTok's official API:

  • user.info.profile – Your TikTok username and account ID, used to identify your account and display it in your AutoVid dashboard.
  • user.info.stats – Your follower count and engagement statistics, used to display performance analytics within AutoVid.
  • video.list – The list of videos on your account, used to avoid duplicate posts and display your publishing history.
  • video.upload – Used to publish AI-generated video content to your TikTok account on your behalf, only when you authorize a scheduled post.

3. How We Use Your Data

AutoVid uses your data exclusively to provide the Service: scheduling and publishing content to your TikTok account. We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal data to any third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your TikTok access credentials are stored securely and encrypted. AutoVid does not store your videos permanently — files are processed and deleted immediately after publishing. We retain only a log of publishing activity (timestamp, post status) for your dashboard.

5. Data Retention

You may disconnect your TikTok account from AutoVid at any time. Upon disconnection, all stored credentials and account data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. You may also revoke AutoVid's access to your TikTok account directly through TikTok's connected apps settings.
